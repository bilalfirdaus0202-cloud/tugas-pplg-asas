Company Profile

Authentic Indonesian Rattan for Malaysian Spaces

Established in 2008, CV. Cirebon Rattan Craft specializes in exporting manau rattan, fitrit rattan, kubu rattan, and teak-combined furniture tailored for tropical climates.

Every piece undergoes kiln drying, anti-fungal & anti-borer treatment, strict quality inspection, and export-grade packaging prior to dispatch from Cirebon to Malaysian ports or airports.
